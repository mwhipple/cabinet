#+title: logic
This will be mostly focused on formal logic, specifically first-order:
the mechanisms through which a range of information can be captured in
formal systems typically consisting of some defined axioms and rules
which allow for theorems to derive additional information from those
axioms.

* Syllogisms

* Quantifiers
